I am a computational neuroscientist with industry and academic experience in neuroimaging and AI/ML, focusing on the clinical translation of imaging biomarkers and prognostic models for neurodegenerative diseases. 

I completed my PhD thesis on prognostic applications for Alzheimer’s disease (AD) using MR imaging and ML techniques under [Dr. Mallar Chakravarty](http://cobralab.ca/members/commander/) at the University of Toronto. Subsequently I worked at the [Allen Institute](https://alleninstitute.org/), developing network spread models of AD pathologies. 

Currently, I am a researcher with [Dr. Jean-Baptiste Poline](https://www.mcgill.ca/neuro/jean-baptiste-poline-phd) in the ORIGAMI lab at McGill University, where I lead the development of several neuroinformatic tools ([Nipoppy](https://nipoppy.readthedocs.io/en/latest/), [QC-Studio](https://qc-studio.readthedocs.io/en/latest/)) and manage their integration in several multi-site initiatives (ENIGMA-PD, NIMHANS, SCAN-D) to ensure reliable and scalable research practices. 

Beyond my core research, I am also involved in open ([OSSIG](https://ossig.netlify.app/)) and sustainable ([SEASIG](https://ohbm-environment.org/)) science groups to promote FAIR principles across the broader research community.
